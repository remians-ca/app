Who We Are

DRMC alumni,
united in Canada.

"To preserve the spirit of DRMC, support our members across Canada, and maintain our bond with the institution and the global Remians family."

We are the official Canadian chapter of the Remians global alumni network — graduates of Dhaka Residential Model College (DRMC), now living and working across Canada. From Toronto to Vancouver, we stay connected, support each other, and celebrate our shared roots.
